# DISCOM Power Procurement Memo (Sample Week Simulation)

*Deliverable D4 — Lead (P1) | CodeSpark 2026*

## 1. Executive Summary
Buying power on a **flat weekly schedule** forces a DISCOM into a lose-lose trap:
- **Over-procurement at night (01:00–06:00 IST)**: Paying capacity/stranded charges on unused surplus.
- **Under-procurement during evening peaks (19:00–23:30 IST)**: Paying punitive real-time Deviation Settlement Mechanism (DSM) tariffs or risking load-shedding.

By scheduling hourly day-ahead purchases at the **calibrated P90 forecast (`schedule_h = P90_h`)**, the DISCOM follows the diurnal load curve while reserving an explicit tail-risk buffer (`P90 - P50`) for peak hours.

## 2. Explicit Cost & Schedule Assumptions
> **Transparency Note**: All tariff parameters below are hackathon assumptions per `Requirements.pdf` Section 6, **not** confidential DISCOM PPA/DSM tariffs. Sensitivity across DSM and stranded rates is reported in `results/procurement_week.json`.

| Parameter | Assumed Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Contracted Day-Ahead Rate** | **Rs 5.0 / kWh** | Base cost of power scheduled in day-ahead market |
| **Under-Procurement (DSM) Rate** | **Rs 12.0 / kWh** | Penalty rate on `max(0, actual - schedule)` shortfall |
| **Over-Procurement Stranded Rate** | **Rs 2.5 / kWh** | Unrecovered capacity charge / resale loss on `max(0, schedule - actual)` |
| **Unit Conversion** | **1,000 kWh / MWh** | 1 MW sustained for 1 hour = 1 MWh = 1,000 kWh |
| **Risk-Averse Schedule** | `schedule_h = P90_h` | Hourly buy at upper 90th-percentile forecast |
| **Point Baseline Schedule** | `schedule_h = P50_h` | Hourly buy at median forecast |
| **Naive Flat Schedule** | `schedule_h = mean(P90)` | Constant 24×7 flat block at week's average P90 |

## 4. Sensitivity to Tariff Assumptions
In `results/procurement_week.json` (`"sensitivity"`), we sweep:
- **DSM rates**: Rs 8.0, 10.0, 12.0, 15.0, 18.0 / kWh
- **Stranded rates**: Rs 1.5, 2.5, 3.5 / kWh

Because `sum(P90_h) == sum(mean(P90))` over the week, total contracted day-ahead cost is **identical** between the P90 hourly schedule and the flat schedule. Every rupee saved comes purely from aligning contracted capacity to the diurnal shape—cutting both night-time stranded surplus and evening DSM penalties—so savings persist positively across the entire tariff grid.
